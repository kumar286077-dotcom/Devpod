FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Devpod free? +

Yes. The core Devpod learning experience is designed to be free without requiring a login.

Do I need to create an account? +

No. Progress, bookmarks and test history can be stored locally in your browser.

Which exams are supported? +

The platform architecture supports UPSSSC PET, recruitment-specific UPSSSC Mains, UP Police, SSC CGL and SSC CHSL.

Does the school section support PCM and PCB? +

Yes. Classes 11 and 12 include dedicated PCM and PCB learning paths with Physics and Chemistry shared between the streams.

Can I use Devpod on mobile? +

Yes. The interface is designed for mobile, tablet and desktop screens.
